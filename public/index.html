ABOUT US

Text messages from unknown numbers, unsolicited calls, and emails that are too good to be true flood the tranquility of our inboxes without permission. These are examples of cyberattacks classified as phishing, which seek to extract your sensitive information while appearing as a trusted source.

In 2025, phishing was the #1 crime according to the FBI, with 191,561 complaints reported. Unfortunately, this statistic cannot account for all attacks. Due to unawareness, many go undetected, and the harsh consequences fall upon those most vulnerable.

We are taking a proactive stand against phishing attacks by developing this platform for users to determine if a piece of media is a potential threat to their security before they consider clicking. As this website is designed for the general public, we have prioritized a simple user interface and have integrated it with an LLM that is capable of identifying common attack symptoms and providing thorough recommendations to keep your data secure..

With the heightened risk of AI-generated attacks, it has become increasingly difficult to identify who or what we can trust. We created Go Phish to be your digital lighthouse and guide you safely across even the roughest seas of the internet.

Tech Stack:
