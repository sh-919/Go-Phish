ABOUT GO PHISH

Text messages from unknown numbers, unsolicited calls, and emails that are too good to be true flood the tranquility of our inboxes without permission. These are examples of cyberattacks classified as phishing, which seek to extract your sensitive information while appearing as a trusted source.

We are taking a proactive stand against phishing attacks by developing this platform for users to determine if a piece of media is a potential threat to their security before they consider clicking. As this website is designed for the general public, we prioritize a simple user interface and use an LLM to identify common attack symptoms and provide recommendations to help keep data secure.

With the heightened risk of AI-generated attacks, it has become increasingly difficult to identify who or what we can trust. We created Go Phish to be your digital lighthouse and guide you safely across even the roughest seas of the internet.
